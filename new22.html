The Three Main Parts:


The brain is divided into three primary sections, each with a specific job:

Cerebrum:


This is the largest part of the brain. It handles the "high-level" stuff like thinking, problem-solving, emotions, and interpreting what you SEE and HEAR. It’s split into two halves (HEMISPHERES).
Cerebellum:

Located at the back of the head, this part is all about movement . It controls your balance, posture, and fine motor coordination (like typing or playing an instrument).
Brainstem:

This connects the brain to the spinal cord. It acts like a HARD-WIRED AUTOPILOT, controlling involuntary survival functions like your heartbeat and breathing.


Together, they ensure that you can process the world around you, interact with it physically, and survive automatically. If the Cerebrum. is the mind., the Cerebellum. is the grace., and the Brainstem. is the life force.
